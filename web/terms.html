About this prototype

NoteIQ displays Microsoft 365 Copilot meeting notes and action items for an internal demonstration. It requires a work account, the applicable Copilot license, administrator permissions, and meeting transcription or recording.

Copilot content may contain mistakes. Review notes and assignments before acting on them. Availability depends on Microsoft services and the computer hosting NoteIQ. Notifications received while the host is unavailable may need manual recovery.
