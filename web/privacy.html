NoteIQ prototype privacy

NoteIQ uses your Microsoft work account to identify you. After you connect, it subscribes to transcript and Copilot insight notifications and stores your name, user ID, transcript text, meeting notes and action items in its database.

Only insights for meetings you organize are displayed to you. NoteIQ downloads transcripts for on-demand viewing. It does not send them to another AI model. If you explicitly connect ClickUp and send action items, NoteIQ sends those action-item details to your selected ClickUp List. NoteIQ sends readiness notifications directly to your Teams Activity feed using its app identity. Notifications contain the meeting topic and readiness text, while transcript and insight content stays inside your authenticated NoteIQ view. No persistent Microsoft user-token cache is stored for notifications. The application uses Microsoft 365, Entra ID and Microsoft Graph. Its hosting location does not determine the residency of those Microsoft services.

Disconnect in Account settings to stop collection and delete your saved NoteIQ cards, transcripts and queued notifications. Already delivered Activity entries are not deleted. Signing out only closes your session; collection continues. Removing the Teams app alone does not disconnect collection in this prototype. Microsoft 365 originals are unaffected.

This is an internal prototype. Ask the person operating this NoteIQ instance about its hosting, backups, and data handling before using sensitive meeting content. Stored cards remain until you disconnect or the operator deletes the local database.
